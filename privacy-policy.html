Privacy Policy

Accevate - Workforce Management Application

Effective Date: November 13, 2026  |  Last Updated: November 13, 2026

Accevate Technologies ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Accevate mobile application ("App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use the App. By using Accevate, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Summary: Accevate is a B2B workforce management app provided to employees by their organizations. We collect location, camera, and profile data strictly for attendance verification, visit tracking, and work documentation. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

1. Information We Collect

a) Personal Information

When your employer registers you on Accevate, we collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Employee ID / login credentials
  • Designation and department
  • Organization / school assignment

b) Location Data

  • Foreground location: Precise GPS coordinates to verify attendance check-in/check-out at assigned work sites
  • Background location: Collected only during active visit journeys (when you start navigation to a client site) to record travel routes and calculate distance
  • Location tracking automatically stops when the visit journey ends
  • We do NOT track your location outside of work activities or after you check out

c) Camera & Photos

  • Selfie photos for attendance verification (check-in/check-out)
  • Site photos for visit documentation
  • Bill/receipt images for expense claims

d) Device Information

  • Battery level (to ensure device availability during field work)
  • Operating system version

e) Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Contacts or address book
  • Call logs or SMS
  • Browsing history
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Health or biometric data

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data solely for workforce management purposes:

Data Type Purpose
Location (foreground) Verify attendance at assigned work sites
Location (background) Record travel routes during active visits for distance calculation and travel verification
Camera / Photos Attendance selfie verification, site documentation, expense receipts
Personal info User identification, work assignments, notifications
Battery level Monitor device availability during field work

3. Background Location Usage

Accevate uses background location only during active visit journeys — when an employee starts navigation to a client site. It is used to record the travel route, calculate distance for reimbursement, and verify that visits were made to correct locations.

  • Background tracking starts only when you begin a visit journey
  • A visible blue indicator appears in your device status bar whenever background tracking is active
  • Tracking automatically stops when the visit journey is completed
  • Location is never collected when you are not on an active visit or after checkout
  • You can end tracking at any time by completing or cancelling the visit

4. Data Storage & Security

  • All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS (TLS 1.2+) connections
  • Personal data is stored on secure servers
  • Access to employee data is restricted to authorized personnel within your organization
  • We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption at rest and in transit
  • Regular security audits are conducted to protect against unauthorized access

5. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do NOT sell, trade, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

Data may be shared only in these limited circumstances:

  • Your employer: The organization that deployed Accevate can access your work-related data (attendance, visits, routes) for workforce management
  • Legal requirements: When required by law, regulation, or legal process
  • Service providers: Trusted infrastructure providers who assist in operating our servers, bound by strict confidentiality agreements

6. Data Retention

  • Your data is retained for the duration of your employment with the organization using Accevate
  • Upon account deletion or employment termination, personal data is deleted within 90 days
  • You may request early deletion by contacting your organization administrator or emailing us
  • Aggregated, anonymized data (which cannot identify you) may be retained for service improvement

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: View all personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Withdraw consent: Revoke location or camera permissions at any time via device settings (note: this may limit App functionality)
  • Data portability: Request a copy of your data in a machine-readable format

To exercise any of these rights, contact your organization's administrator or email us at support@accevate.in.

8. Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time by:

  • Using the "Delete Account" option in the App (Profile > Delete Account)
  • Emailing support@accevate.in with your registered details

Upon receiving a deletion request, we will permanently remove your personal data within 90 days. Your employer will be notified of the account deletion.

9. Children's Privacy

Accevate is a workforce management tool intended for use by adults (18 years and above) who are employed by organizations using the Accevate platform. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 18. If we become aware that a child's data has been collected, we will promptly delete it.

10. Cookies & Tracking

The Accevate mobile app does not use cookies, advertising trackers, or analytics SDKs that track users across apps or websites. We do not participate in cross-app tracking.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, operational, or regulatory reasons. When we make changes:

  • The "Last Updated" date at the top will be revised
  • Significant changes will be communicated through the App or via your organization
  • Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy
